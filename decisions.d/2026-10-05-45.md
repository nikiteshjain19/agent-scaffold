# D-45 — 2026-10-05 — `PROJECT.md` declares the merge gate's inputs and never predicts its outcome

- **Decision:** `PROJECT.md` declares the merge gate's inputs and never predicts its outcome. The
  risk list, the green signal and the size threshold decide, through the two-tier gate in
  `CLAUDE.md` §6. The "Who merges" row points at that gate. No sentence in `PROJECT.md` states how
  many PRs reach a human.
- **Context:** #45. `PROJECT.md` restated the gate's outcome in three places: the "Who merges" row
  ("A human, for nearly every PR"), the "Green signal" paragraph ("the risk list below still sends
  nearly every PR to a human"), and the paragraph after the risk list ("That covers nearly every
  file here"). The owner's principle, in their words on 2026-10-05: "there should not be any
  specific entry in project.md that should be driven by the risk list … there should not be any
  additional lines saying oh escalate everything in this repository to me."

  **Why a prediction is a second copy that drifts.** `PROJECT.md` declares the gate's inputs, and
  `CLAUDE.md` §6 turns them into an outcome. A sentence that states the outcome is a second copy of
  that decision, written by hand. It is true only while the list stays as it is. The day a path
  leaves the list, the prediction is false, and nothing that edits the list is obliged to find it.
  The risk list alone now decides, and the only text about the outcome is the rule that produces
  it. The paragraph after the risk list keeps the one thing a reader needs that the list cannot
  say: why it is broad here, which is that this project's product is its own governance. The
  remark that a consuming repository declares a narrower list was dropped, because `CLAUDE.md` §0
  topic 7 already proposes a consuming project's default list.

  **The two stale lines PR #42 left for a follow-up.** PR #42 (#8, `a34074e`) changed `CLAUDE.md`
  §11 rule 9 to re-run the pre-PR gate after every sync with the default branch, a rebase before
  the first push or a merge after it. `PROJECT.md`'s "Pre-PR gate" line still said "after any
  rebase", and its "Who merges" row still described one tier. PR #42's body named both as left
  false, and D-8's **Affects** field records the same. This change fixes both.

  **Why no `Supersedes` and no `Corrects`.** `CLAUDE.md` §7 asks whether an earlier decision still
  binds. D-3, D-8 and D-35 each record, in their Context, that nearly every path here is
  risk-listed, or that most PRs here reach a human. Those are records of the list's effect when
  they were written, and they stay true while the list is unchanged. None of them decided that
  `PROJECT.md` should carry a prediction, so no earlier decision is switched off or rescoped.
  Neither relation applies, and no earlier entry is edited.
- **Affects:** `PROJECT.md` ("Toolchain" → "Who merges"; "Merge gate" → "Green signal"; "Merge
  gate" → "Risk-list paths"; "Build / lint / test commands"); D-8; #8; PR #42; #45.
